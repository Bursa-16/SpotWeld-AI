# Engineering Engine

## Inputs
- Material family and subtype
- 2T/3T/4T stack
- Individual sheet thicknesses
- Coating and adhesive
- Current, time, force
- Tip diameter
- Squeeze and hold
- Cooling flow and temperature
- Shunt condition

## Outputs
- Recommended ranges
- Nugget estimate
- Weld-lobe candidates
- Model comparison
- Rule compliance
- Potential failure probabilities
- Prioritized corrective actions

## Safety principle
The engine is decision support. Physical validation and authorized engineering
approval remain mandatory.
